VOCABULARY
l'annonce (lah-NOHNSS) - ad, listing
le loyer (luh lwah-YAY) - rent
les charges (lay SHARZH) - utility costs, fees
par mois (pahr MWAH) - per month
mètres carrés (MEH-truh kah-RAY) - square metres
visiter (vee-zee-TAY) - to visit, view
louer (loo-AY) - to rent
meublé (muh-BLAY) - furnished
comprises (kohn-PREEZ) - included

GRAMMAR
Rent and numbers - Say rents with the hundreds you learned: six cents euros, huit cents euros, mille euros. Remember that cent takes an s only when it ends the number: six cents, but six cent cinquante. Add par mois to say per month.
Reading a small ad - Ads use short forms: T3 or trois-pièces, 40 m², meublé, CC, proche métro. Ask Je peux visiter ? to arrange a viewing and quand ? for the time.
