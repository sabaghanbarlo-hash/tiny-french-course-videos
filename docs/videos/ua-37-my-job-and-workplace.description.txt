VOCABULARY
l'agence de voyage (lah-ZHAHNSS duh vwah-YAHZH) - travel agency
le collègue / la collègue (luh koh-LEHG) - colleague
la réunion (lah ray-ew-NYOHN) - meeting
le patron / la patronne (luh pah-TROHN) - boss
le travail (luh trah-VAHY) - work, job
intéressant / intéressante (an-tay-reh-SAHN) - interesting
fatigant / fatigante (fah-tee-GAHN) - tiring
l'heure (LUHR) - hour, time
combien de (kohn-BYAN duh) - how much, how many

GRAMMAR
Where do you work? - Use dans for a place inside which you work, à for a town or a named place and chez for a company or a person. Ask Tu travailles où ?
Son, sa, ses - His / her changes with the noun, not with the owner: son plus masculine, sa plus feminine, ses plus plural. Before a vowel use son: son amie. Compare with mon, ma, mes from Unit 2.
