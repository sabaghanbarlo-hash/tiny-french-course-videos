VOCABULARY
zéro (zay-ROH) - zero
un, deux, trois (uhn, duh, TRWAH) - one, two, three
cinq (SANK) - five
huit (WEET) - eight
dix (DEESS) - ten
onze (OHNZ) - eleven
vingt (VAN) - twenty
quel âge as-tu ? (kehl AHZH ah TEW) - how old are you? (friendly)
l'an / les ans (lahn / lay ZAHN) - year(s) of age
le numéro de téléphone (luh new-may-ROH duh tay-lay-FOHN) - phone number
ça s'écrit comment ? (sah say-KREE koh-MAHN) - how do you spell that?
arobase (ah-roh-BAHZ) - at sign (@)

GRAMMAR
Age with avoir - In French you do not "are" an age, you "have" it. Say J'ai vingt ans, with avoir: j'ai, tu as, il / elle a. Never forget ans: J'ai vingt sounds unfinished.
Phone numbers in pairs - French phone numbers are read in groups of two digits. Say the zero first, then each pair as a number: 06 12 34 56 78 is zéro six, douze, trente-quatre, cinquante-six, soixante-dix-huit.
Spelling and e-mail - Ask Ça s'écrit comment ? to have a word spelled. For an e-mail, say arobase for @ and point for the dot: nom arobase mail point com.
