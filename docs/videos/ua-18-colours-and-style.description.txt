VOCABULARY
rouge (ROOZH) - red
bleu / bleue (BLUH) - blue
vert / verte (VEHR / VEHRT) - green
jaune (ZHOHN) - yellow
noir / noire (NWAHR) - black
blanc / blanche (BLAHN / BLAHNSH) - white
gris / grise (GREE / GREEZ) - grey
marron (mah-ROHN) - brown
foncé / clair (fohn-SAY / KLEHR) - dark / light

GRAMMAR
Colours agree - Colours are adjectives: add -e for feminine and -s for plural. Some change more: blanc becomes blanche, noir becomes noire. Colours come after the noun: une robe verte, des chaussures noires.
Colours that never change - Marron and orange never change, even in the plural: des chaussures marron, une veste orange. Add foncé or clair for shades: un bleu foncé, un vert clair, and these stay the same too.
