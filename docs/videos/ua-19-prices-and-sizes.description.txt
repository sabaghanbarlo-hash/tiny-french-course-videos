VOCABULARY
la taille (lah TAHY) - size (clothes)
coûter (koo-TAY) - to cost
cher / chère (SHEHR) - expensive
pas cher (pah SHEHR) - cheap
en promotion (ahn proh-moh-SYOHN) - on sale
par carte (pahr KART) - by card
en espèces (ahn ehs-PEHSS) - in cash
cent, mille (SAHN, MEEL) - one hundred, one thousand
deux cents (duh SAHN) - two hundred

GRAMMAR
Hundreds and thousands - Cent takes an s when it is multiplied and ends the number: deux cents, trois cents. It loses the s when another number follows: deux cent cinquante. Mille never takes an s: deux mille. Say prices with euros and centimes: trente-neuf euros vingt.
Le, la, les as an object - To avoid repeating the noun, replace it with le, la or les placed before the verb: Je prends ce pull becomes Je le prends. Vous avez la veste ? Oui, nous l'avons. Il is for the subject; le, la, les is for the thing you take.
