VOCABULARY
le week-end (luh wee-KEHND) - weekend
la semaine prochaine (lah suh-MEHN proh-SHEHN) - next week
rester (rehs-TAY) - to stay
voir (VWAHR) - to see
revenir (ruh-vuh-NEER) - to come back
la série (lah say-REE) - series, TV show
le programme (luh proh-GRAHM) - plan, programme
vendredi soir (vahn-druh-DEE SWAHR) - Friday evening
demain (duh-MAN) - tomorrow

GRAMMAR
The near future with aller - Use the present of aller plus an infinitive to say what you are going to do: je vais voir, tu vas prendre, il va rester, nous allons partir, vous allez travailler, ils vont revenir. In a negative sentence, ne pas goes around aller: je ne vais pas partir.
Time words for plans - Put the time word at the start or the end: demain, après-demain, ce week-end, la semaine prochaine, le mois prochain. With days, use no preposition: samedi, vendredi soir, dimanche matin.
